# Incompressible and already-compressed product protocol

## Purpose

Freeze the product gate for inputs that a lossless compressor should usually
store: high-entropy bytes, valid already-compressed streams, deceptive mixtures
of compressible and incompressible regions, and random bytes carrying plausible
format magic. The machine-readable authority is
`config/incompressible-precompressed-gates-v1.json`.

This category cannot honestly target a universal 5% size win. By pigeonhole
counting, an injective lossless mapping cannot make every fixed-length string
shorter; some inputs must stay the same size or expand. Axiom succeeds here by
matching the complete equally framed store artifact byte-for-byte, avoiding the
cost of an inappropriate strong codec, and preserving exact bounded streaming
behavior. A store tie is a safety success, never a compression-ratio win.

## Frozen development families

### Generated high entropy

`shake256-uniform` uses counter-mode 1 MiB SHAKE-256 blocks. Each block is
`SHAKE256(domain || 0x00 || item_id || uint64_le(block_index))` at ten exact
boundary and large-item sizes from 0 bytes through 64 MiB, with domain
`compression-lab/incompressible-development/v1`. Generated bytes are released
as CC0-1.0. The generator, byte counts, and output SHA-256 values must be
checked into the acquisition receipt; Python `random.Random` is not admitted
as the formal high-entropy source.

`deceptive-region-order` builds exact 64 MiB items from random, zero,
repeated-text, and uint32-counter regions. Every first/middle/last placement and
alternating 1 MiB ordering needed to defeat prefix-only, suffix-only, and
three-point sampling is included. `magic-spoofed-random` prefixes SHAKE-256
bytes with plausible gzip, ZIP, PNG, JPEG, PDF, Zstandard, XZ, and 7-Zip magic;
no route may trust a filename or magic value without measured benefit.

Each complete canonical bundle is encoded independently with frozen gzip-9,

precompressing it does not turn it into fresh validation evidence.

`scripts/prepare-incompressible-precompressed-execution.py` converts the
machine protocol and tracked licensed acquisition receipt into an immutable
49-task plan:
31 generated items and 18 licensed precompression/round-trip tasks. It requires
a completely clean commit and records zero Axiom wins. The standalone

The plan is a pre-execution lock, not a corpus or benchmark result.

`scripts/build-incompressible-precompressed-development.py` is the resumable,

source bundles or their ignored corpus manifest. If derivative payloads are not
redistributed, an independent reproducer must rebuild them from the frozen

## Frozen unseen splits

Only the five declared development families may influence the implementation
or thresholds. Public validation remains unopened and unselected until the
candidate is frozen. It will use at least four future lineage-disjoint official
source-release and Wikimedia publisher artifacts absent from development,
derive high-entropy seeds from their publisher digests, construct the same
precompression-format matrix, and score the unchanged candidate exactly once.

Private holdout remains inaccessible. An independent holder will select at
least four licensed real-world compressed-media/archive families plus a secret
high-entropy seed after the implementation, commands, and gates are frozen.
Only the unchanged one-shot result and legally redistributable evidence may be
returned. Neither a public-validation nor private-holdout family may be used to
diagnose or repair the candidate that consumed it.

## Candidate and comparison boundary

The category candidate is the complete Axiom selector, frame, store/direct
route, decoder, and integrity path—not a naked `memcpy`. Compare it against the
same-host roster of Store, LZ4-1, gzip-1/9, bzip2-9, Zstandard-1/3/9,
Brotli-1/11, XZ/LZMA2-0/9e, and 7-Zip/LZMA2-9. All codec versions, binaries,
commands, threads, and complete archive accounting are frozen in the execution
receipt.

The equally framed store comparator uses the same Axiom format version,
headers, source digest, segment records, checksums, and footer as the candidate,
with every recipe forced to store. Therefore the no-expansion comparison does
not hide Axiom framing on one side. The final candidate artifact may contain
**zero bytes more** than this comparator. Full-output comparison must override
a wrong sample decision before publication; an unavailable, failed, timed-out,
or unsafe baseline is reported as such and never becomes an Axiom win.

The universal preselector may inspect at most 262,144 source bytes regardless
of input length. Format magic alone cannot select a codec. Codec-internal
stream processing may consume the full input after selection, but the evidence
used to choose among category specialists and store is bounded independently of
file size. Every sampled offset and byte count is included in the receipt.

### Existing fallback eligibility audit

The alpha adaptive-v3 `.clab` fallback is safety evidence, not the candidate
for this gate. Its current selector samples 32 KiB independently from every
1 MiB segment, so selector evidence grows with input length instead of staying
within the fixed 262,144-byte ceiling. On data that ultimately routes to store,
it also attempts sample compression for every segment and may attempt a
whole-input Zstandard encoding before making the size-safe fallback. Finally,
its Python byte API materializes complete inputs and outputs and therefore
cannot prove the native 1/4 GiB streaming, 64 MiB window, or throughput gates.

The existing one-segment adaptive-v3 store artifact does demonstrate the
required accounting principle: its frame header, segment count, segment
record, checksum, source digest, and raw payload are all counted. A successor
may reuse that principle, but it must use one native streaming envelope for
both the forced-store comparator and selected candidate, bound selector
evidence globally, and avoid strong-codec work on confidently incompressible
inputs. No readiness credit is awarded by this static audit.

## Operational and large-file gate

Use one warmup and five cold-process measured repetitions, one codec thread,
complete-source-bytes/wall-time throughput, and worst child peak RSS. The final
native store route must meet all of these predeclared thresholds:

| Gate | Requirement |
| --- | ---: |
| complete candidate expansion | 0 bytes above equally framed store |
| selector evidence | at most 262,144 input bytes, independent of file size |
| selector overhead | at most 5.00% above native equally framed store copy/hash time |
| average compression throughput | at least 1,000 MB/s |
| minimum compression repetition | at least 750 MB/s |
| average decompression throughput | at least 1,500 MB/s |
| minimum decompression repetition | at least 1,000 MB/s |
| compression peak RSS | at most 128 MiB |
| decompression peak RSS | at most 128 MiB |
| streaming window | at most 64 MiB |

The 1 GiB and 4 GiB gates use ordinary generated files, never sparse-file
logical sizes or repeated in-memory byte objects. The runner streams source,
candidate, and restoration digests in fixed chunks and measures the complete
CLI path. It must demonstrate bounded memory, exact restoration, deterministic
artifacts, no stale/partial output after failure, and behavior beyond 32-bit
length boundaries. Python measurements are diagnostic only; only supported
native standalone distributions can satisfy the final speed and memory gate.

## Integrity and portability

For every item, five measured complete artifacts must be byte-identical and
every decode must restore the exact source size and SHA-256. The decoder accepts
an explicit maximum output size, checks declared sizes before allocation, and
streams payload verification. Test every one-bit mutation of the fixed header,
representative segment-header/checksum mutations, payload mutation, every
truncated fixed-header length, payload truncation, append, forged segment count,
forged uint64 size, and trailing bytes. Rejected decodes retain no destination.

At least one second supported operating system and architecture must decode the
exact retained artifacts. Portability evidence includes package identity,
executable bytes and SHA-256, platform, architecture, and result digests.

## Publication

After a complete gate, publish an immutable five-file bundle: `README.md`,
`comparison.json`, `comparison.svg`, privacy-safe `evidence.json`, and
`receipt.json`. The standardized chart retains every tested standard plus
Axiom and reports complete bytes/ratio, compression speed, decompression speed,
peak memory, exactness, determinism, corruption and portability status, runner
comparability, whether Axiom beat each row, split stage, and the exact claim
ceiling. Store-size ties are labeled ties, not wins. Pending, unavailable, and
failed cells remain visible.

The offline verifier must reconstruct the chart and gate decision from public
receipts without the private holdout, raw licensed payloads, or machine-local
paths. No publication may claim a category pass until the large-file and
second-platform gates also pass.
